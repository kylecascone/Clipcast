Get up and running in under 5 minutes

1

Connect Your Accounts

Go to the Accounts page and connect your TikTok, YouTube, and Instagram accounts. You'll need your session cookies from each platform.

Tip: Use browser dev tools → Application → Cookies to find your sessionid cookie
2

Refresh Your Clip Pool

Click Refresh Pool on the Dashboard. ClipCast will automatically discover viral clips from Reddit, YouTube, and Twitch and score them by viral potential.

Tip: Run a refresh every day to keep your pool fresh. Aim for 200+ clips in the pool.
3

Set Your Posting Schedule

Go to the Schedule page and add time slots for each day. Set which creator or content type you want for each slot, which platforms to post to, and a minimum quality score.

Tip: Start with 2–3 posts per day. Consistency beats volume when building a new account.
4

Run a Test

Click Test Run on the Dashboard. ClipCast will process 3 clips without posting them so you can review the output videos before going live.

Tip: Check the processed videos in the Recent Videos section on the Dashboard.
5

Go Live

Click Post Now to run the full pipeline and post immediately, or let the schedule run automatically at your set times.

Tip: Monitor your Analytics page after the first week to see which content is performing best.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I refresh the clip pool?
Refresh once or twice a day. ClipCast automatically deduplicates clips so running it more often just keeps the pool topped up with fresh viral content.
How many posts per day should I schedule?
Start with 3–4 posts per day spread out across morning, afternoon, and evening. TikTok and YouTube Shorts reward consistency. Once your accounts are established you can scale to 6–8 per day.
What minimum quality score should I use?
Set minimum score to 50 when starting out so you have enough clips to fill your schedule. As your pool grows you can raise it to 65–70 to only post the best content.
Will my accounts get banned for posting clips?
ClipCast includes a DMCA music detector that flags clips with copyrighted music before posting. Clips with harmful or inappropriate content are filtered automatically. Still, always review your content and follow each platform's community guidelines.
How does the AI quality scoring work?
Every clip gets scored 0–100 based on viral potential (views, upvotes, source), AI quality analysis (self-contained, entertaining, TikTok fit), and freshness. The AI also rewrites clip titles to be more engaging and TikTok-ready.
