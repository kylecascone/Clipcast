Get up and running in under 5 minutes. Follow these steps to start auto-posting viral clips.

1

Connect Your YouTube Account

Click Accounts in the sidebar, enter your OAuth credentials from Google Cloud Console, then click Connect with Google. ClipCast will authenticate using the official YouTube Data API v3 and display your channel name once connected.

2

Set Your Content Categories & Schedule

Go to Schedule and create posting slots. Pick your content categories (Gaming, Sports, Fails, etc.), set the posting time, choose your target platform, and optionally pin a specific creator. Save your slots — ClipCast will post exactly one clip per slot, never more.

3

ClipCast Discovers Trending Creators Automatically

Every 6 hours, ClipCast scans Twitch for the top 50 live streamers and cross-references Reddit viral posts (1000+ upvotes). The highest-scoring clips surface automatically in your Clip Pool. You can also click Fetch New Clips on the Clip Pool page to refresh on demand.

4

Videos Are Edited Automatically

Each clip is processed through a full editing pipeline: vertical cropping or blurred background layout (auto-detected), animated word-by-word captions transcribed with Whisper AI, a hook text card overlay, and branding — all completed in 2–3 minutes. The final file is a 1080×1920 MP4 ready for upload.

5

Posts Go Out at Your Scheduled Times

When a schedule slot fires, ClipCast selects the best matching clip from the pool (filtered by category, min score, and creator preference), processes it, and uploads automatically. Check Analytics after a few days to see which content is getting the most views and engagement.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does ClipCast find clips?
ClipCast combines three discovery sources: the Twitch Trending API (top 50 live streamers by viewer count), Reddit viral posts filtered to 1000+ upvotes from gaming and entertainment subreddits, and YouTube Shorts discovery across 20+ content categories. All clips are de-duplicated and scored before entering your pool.
Does it post automatically?
Yes, once you configure a schedule. Posts go out exactly at your slot times — 1 clip per slot, never more. If no matching clip is found for a slot (e.g. minimum score too high, no clips from the specified creator), that slot is skipped and logged. You can also manually trigger a post from the Dashboard at any time.
How many posts per day should I use?
Start with 2–3 per day. TikTok and YouTube Shorts algorithms reward consistent daily posting over high volume. Spread your slots across morning, afternoon, and evening for best reach. Once your accounts are established and your clip pool is deep, you can scale to 5–6 per day.
What's a "Min Score" in the schedule?
Each clip receives a quality score from 0–100 based on view velocity (40%), clip duration fit (35%), and audio quality (25%). A bonus of up to 10 points is added for clips that match your target category. Set Min Score to filter out lower-quality clips — start at 40–50 while your pool is small, then raise to 60–70 as it grows.
Will my accounts get banned?
ClipCast uses official APIs only — YouTube Data API v3 and TikTok Official Content Posting API. No session cookies, no browser automation, no third-party scraping. This means you operate within each platform's terms of service. Note: always include proper attribution (creator name + source) and respect creator opt-out requests via the Blocked Creators list.
Can I use specific creators only?
Yes — when creating a schedule slot, set a creator name (Twitch username or YouTube channel) and ClipCast will only post clips from that creator in that slot. Leave it blank to pull the best clip from the entire pool matching your other filters (category, min score, platform).
What video formats work for manual uploads?
Use the Editor page to submit a Twitch clip URL or YouTube video URL for manual processing. ClipCast will download, edit, and add captions automatically. Direct file uploads support MP4, MOV, and AVI up to 500 MB. Vertical (9:16) clips work best — horizontal clips are automatically converted with a blurred background fill layout.
How do I see performance data?
Go to the Analytics page. Data populates after your first posted clips — views and engagement metrics are pulled from the YouTube and TikTok APIs. The dashboard also shows today's total views, average engagement rate, and your best-performing platform at a glance.
