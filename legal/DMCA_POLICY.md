# ClipCast Studio — DMCA Policy

**Version 1.0 — Effective February 2026**

---

## Our Commitment

ClipCast Studio respects the intellectual property rights of all creators and copyright
holders. We are committed to responding to all legitimate DMCA takedown requests promptly
and in good faith. If you are a content creator or rights holder and you have concerns
about how ClipCast Studio processes your content, we want to hear from you.

---

## Reporting Copyright Infringement

If you believe that content processed through ClipCast Studio infringes your copyright,
please send a DMCA notice to:

**Email:** `dmca@clipcast.studio` *(replace with your actual contact email)*

Your notice must include all of the following:

1. **Identification of the work** — Describe the copyrighted work that you believe has
   been infringed (title, URL of original, etc.).

2. **Identification of the infringing content** — Provide the specific URL on TikTok,
   YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, or wherever the infringing content appears.

3. **Your contact information** — Full name, mailing address, phone number, and email.

4. **Good faith statement** — "I have a good faith belief that the use of the material
   described above is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law."

5. **Accuracy statement** — "I swear, under penalty of perjury, that the information in
   this notification is accurate and that I am the copyright owner or am authorized to
   act on behalf of the copyright owner."

6. **Signature** — Your physical or electronic signature.

---

## Our Response Process

Upon receiving a valid DMCA notice, we will:

1. **Acknowledge receipt within 48 hours**
2. Review the claim and contact the operator who posted the content
3. Remove or disable access to the content if the claim is valid
4. Notify the operator of the removal and their right to submit a counter-notice

---

## Counter-Notices

If you are a ClipCast Studio operator and believe your content was wrongly removed,
you may submit a counter-notice. Counter-notices must comply with 17 U.S.C. § 512(g)(3)
and include:

1. Your physical or electronic signature
2. Identification of the material that was removed
3. A statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good faith belief the material
   was removed by mistake or misidentification
4. Your name, address, phone number, and a statement consenting to jurisdiction

Counter-notices will be forwarded to the original complainant within 10 business days.

---

## Repeat Infringers

Operators who repeatedly infringe copyright will have their access to ClipCast Studio
terminated in accordance with our policies and applicable law.

---

## Opt-Out for Creators

If you are a streamer or content creator who does not want your content processed by
ClipCast Studio operators, you can request opt-out by contacting us at the email above.
ClipCast Studio maintains an opt-out list (`legal/blocked_creators.yaml`) and we will
add your username promptly upon request.

---

## Good Faith

This policy is intended to be a practical, fair tool for resolving legitimate copyright
disputes quickly. We are not a legal adversary — we want to work with rights holders
to protect creative work while supporting operators who follow the rules.
